Twilight Comforts

As evening falls, the sky transforms into a canvas of deep oranges, pinks, and purples. The farmhouse lights turn on, casting a warm glow across the yard. The farm animals settle into their beds, and the frantic energy of the day finally slows to a quiet hum.

Barnaby takes his favorite spot on the wooden front porch right next to Farmer John’s rocking chair. With a heavy sigh of contentment, he rests his chin on his paws. His eyes slowly close as the crickets begin their nightly song, knowing the farm is safe, sound, and ready for tomorrow.
