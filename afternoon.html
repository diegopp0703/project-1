The Lost Chick

By afternoon, the farm buzzes with heat and activity. While checking on the chicken coop, Barnaby hears a faint, distressed peeping sound coming from the tall weeds near the old tractor. His ears perk up instantly.

Stepping carefully so as not to scare the little creature, Barnaby noses his way through the wildflowers. There, tangled in a patch of briars, is a tiny yellow chick that had wandered away from its mother. Barnaby gently nudges the brambles aside with his paw, creating a clear path, and patiently guides the frightened chick all the way back to the safety of the coop.
