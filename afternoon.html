The Lost Chicken

By afternoon, the farm buzzes with heat and activity. While checking on the chicken coop, Barnaby hears a distressed peeping sound coming from the tall weeds by the old tractor in the distance. His ears stand up instantly.

Stepping carefully to not scare the little chicken, Barnaby noses his way through the tall weeds. There, tangled in a patch of briars, is a tiny yellow chicken that wandered away from its mother. Barnaby gently nudges the brambles aside with his paw, creating a clear path, and guides the frightened chicken back to the safety with the coop.
