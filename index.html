Meet Barnaby

Welcome to Willow Farm, a rolling stretch of green valleys, ancient oak trees, and lively animals. At the heart of this beautiful homestead is Barnaby, a golden retriever with floppy ears and a heart too big for his chest. Barnaby isn't just a pet; he is the self-appointed guardian, supervisor, and best friend of every living soul on the farm.

Every morning brings a new adventure, and Barnaby takes his job very seriously. Follow his journey through a typical day on the farm using the navigation links above.
