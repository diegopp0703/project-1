Meet Barnaby

Welcome to Willow Farm, a scenic view of green valleys, big trees, and farm animals. At the heart of this beautiful farmhouse is Barnaby, a golden retriever with floppy ears and a heart full of energy. Barnaby isn't just a dog; he is the guardian, supervisor, and best friend of every living soul on the farm.

Every morning brings a new task, and Barnaby handles his job with responsibility. Come along on a journey through a typical day on the farm using the navigation links above.
