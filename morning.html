The Sun Rises and the Sheep Move

As the sun rises over the farm hills, Barnaby is already on the move. The morning mist hangs low over the damp grass when Barnaby's owner Kevin opens the barn doors. With a sharp bark and a wagging tail, Barnaby runs toward the farm field.

His first task of the day is helping guide the sheep out to the meadow. The sheep can be stubborn at times, but they respect Barnaby. He runs wide circles around the flock, gently nudging the stragglers back into flock. By the time the sun is high enough to warm the dew away, the flock is safely eating grass, and Barnaby sits patiently at the gate, keeping watch.
