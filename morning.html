The Sun Rises and the Sheep Move

As the first rays of dawn break over the eastern hills, Barnaby is already on his feet. The morning mist still hangs low over the damp grass when Farmer John opens the barn doors. With a sharp bark and a wagging tail, Barnaby bounds toward the pasture.

His first task of the day is helping guide the sheep out to the eastern meadow. The sheep can be stubborn, but they respect Barnaby. He runs wide circles around the flock, gently nudging the stragglers back into line. By the time the sun is high enough to warm the dew away, the flock is safely grazing, and Barnaby sits proudly at the gate, keeping watch.
